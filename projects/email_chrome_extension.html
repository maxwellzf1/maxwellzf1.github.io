Email Reply Generation with LLM's

Dec 17, 2025

This project was inspired by someone's Hackathon at CMU. I recall attending the awards ceremony and seeing a group of 3-4 students who created a Google Chrome extension using ChatGPT to construct a reply to emails.

I've always struggled with writing and would need some time to craft a polite and delicate email. I thought I could borrow the idea and write my own Chrome extension.
