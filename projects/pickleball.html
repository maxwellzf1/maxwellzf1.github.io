Pickleball Flight

Dec 17, 2025

This project is a class project for the CMU course 15-418 Parallel Computer Architecture and Programming. The course covers basics of parallel programming, including threading, SIMD programming, and GPU programming. The course also covers system-level issues of parallelism, including synchronization and cache coherency.

Our project involved rendering a ball (supposed to be a pickleball) that collides with air particles that hinder its movement. On CPU, we can only render up to 3000 air particles without much stalling. With CUDA, we can render around 1 million particles. The general idea to improve speedup was creating spatial grids, mapping each particle to a thread, and only checking neighboring particles for each thread.
